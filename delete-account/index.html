Delete your cloud account

You can permanently delete your Nido Firebase account and synced cloud data directly in the app.

Delete from Nido Baby

  1. Open Nido Baby.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete cloud account.
  4. Review the confirmation and tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm with your device biometric or PIN/password.

What is deleted

This permanently removes the Firebase Authentication account used by Nido and its synced Firestore baby profiles, photos, and care-log data, including the change history of edited and deleted entries.

What stays on your device

Your local encrypted Nido database remains on the device after cloud-account deletion. This lets you keep using Nido offline. To remove local data too, clear Nido Baby’s app storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

If you cannot access the app

Email eze.os93@gmail.com from the Google account that was connected to Nido Baby and request account deletion. Account ownership must be verified before cloud data can be deleted.
